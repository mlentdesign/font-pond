Font Pond

Privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

Font Pond is a tool for exploring, comparing and pairing fonts, at fontpond.melware.art.

What Font Pond handles

  • Your choices. Your history, selections and settings are saved in your browser only. Font Pond keeps no other information about you on a server.
  • No account. There is no sign-in, and your name and email are not collected.
  • How the site is used. Google Analytics records which pages are opened, the kind of device and browser, and an approximate location from your IP address. Your name and email are never sent to it.
  • Other services. Font files are served from the site itself. Nothing else is loaded from third parties apart from Google Analytics.

There is no advertising, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Deleting your data

To have anything you submitted removed, contact the developer through the options at melware.art.

No AI training

You may not use this site’s content, data or design — including its writing, images, layout and visual style — to train, fine-tune or evaluate artificial intelligence or machine-learning models, and no one else is permitted to do so either. Known AI-training crawlers are blocked, and robots.txt and response headers say the same. AI assistants that read a page on your behalf to help you navigate it are welcome.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
